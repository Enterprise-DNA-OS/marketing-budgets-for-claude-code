# Target decision: Marmind marketing budgets

Checked 2026-11-15. The registry was reviewed before registration. Marketing budget management was not an existing build family. Consumer-product licensing, built earlier in this run, was excluded. The finished FADEL LicenSee rebuild supplied the quality reference, not its domain code.

## Annual bill evidence and limits

US$20,400 a year is twelve times the historical US$1,700 monthly Premium listing in [FinancesOnline](https://reviews.financesonline.com/p/marmind/), which labels it annual billing for up to twenty members. It is a third-party historical listing. It is not a verified paid invoice, a current vendor price, or evidence that a particular NZ or AU business pays that amount.

[Marmind's current pricing](https://www.marmind.com/pricing) names Essentials, Professional and Enterprise, all privately quoted. The public page therefore contains no numeric Marmind price. Historical plan names and figures are excluded from the page and the sourced prices list. The thesis needs the operator's actual quote before any savings comparison.

No Marmind reference was found in the local customer and CRM context searched. Live CRM access was not used. Buyer fit is an inference about owner-led groups reviewing marketing spend. No claim of an existing customer relationship is made. Search results contain alternatives lists and comparison pages, but keyword volume and ranking difficulty were not verified.
